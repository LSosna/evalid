# Changelog

## 0.3.4 — 2026-09-06

Cut in response to an independent QA/QC and hostile-referee review of v0.3.3.
Every finding it raised is either fixed here or explicitly acknowledged as a
limitation. All corrections are logged in `CORRECTIONS.csv`.

### Protocol

- **§2 now requires an independent timestamp on the pre-registration**, before
  data are loaded. A hash inside a manifest built after the results proves
  internal consistency, not chronology. The GW-SRAG audit does not meet this
  requirement and the paper says so.
- **§5.2 now requires a finder kind per correction entry** — author, person, or
  AI model run against the artefact — requires quoted counts to be derived from
  the log, and forbids counting unresolved provenance as human review.

### Fixed

- `audits/002-mmlu/CORRIGENDUM.md` §8 carried a paragraph that was written by
  an assistant addressing the author, and left in a document published in the
  author's voice. It also referred to private notes and to matters outside the
  scope of an audit report. Removed. It is present in the Zenodo archives of
  v0.3.2 and v0.3.4-as-first-tagged, which can be superseded by a new version
  but not withdrawn.
- The correction count did not reconcile: 14/11 in prose, 10 rows in the table,
  16 findings implied by the corrigendum. The log is now `CORRECTIONS.csv` with
  a stated counting rule; `CORRECTIONS.md` and `corrections_summary.json` are
  generated from it by `evalid corrections`, and prose quotes the derived
  totals.
- `reproduce.py --all` claimed to regenerate every results file. It wrote four
  of five, and overwrote `t3_ff.json` with a schema different from the shipped
  one, breaking `manifest verify`. All five are now generated by shipped code
  via the new `code/ff_engine.py`.
- `t4_rest.json` shipped a `separability` block holding the **retracted v0 P6
  values**. This is finding R2-10 (a second, unbound source of truth)
  recurring, carrying numbers the corrigendum withdraws. Replaced by a pointer
  to `p6_corrected.json`.
- The BBH condition number was reported as 1.95e16 from an orphaned file; the
  anchored source gives 2.55e16. Gate verdict unchanged.
- The SPARC-λ residual is 5.30σ, not 5.27σ; the "reproduces to five decimal
  places" claim applies to the fitting factor, not to the σ derived from 1−FF.
- "Published" was used for the v0 report and figure, which were never public.
  Now "as shipped in v0".
- `evalid conform` printed only the protocol version while the package reported
  a different one; it now prints both.
- BibTeX: `{L}orentz` braced, `journal` field added to the GWTC-3 entry,
  `\date{\today}` replaced with a fixed date so arXiv rebuilds do not re-date
  the paper.
- Removed a committed patch file describing its own commit, and an unused
  `\todo` macro.
- `paper/README_arxiv.md` still said "NOT READY TO POST" against blockers
  resolved by excision. Rewritten.

### Acknowledged, not fixed

- **The pre-registration for the GW-SRAG audit has no independent timestamp.**
  Stated in the paper as a limitation of the present work.
- **The audited SRAG/SRLG corpus is not independently citable**, so P2's
  verdicts are reproducible from the formulas but not falsifiable against the
  source. Stated in the paper.
- **Twenty correction-log entries carry `finder_kind: external-unspecified`.**
  Until each is resolved to a person or an AI model, the external-finder ratio
  is not a claim about human peer review, and the tooling says so on every run.

### Added

- `evalid corrections` — derives the counts from the log and refuses to let
  prose drift from the table.
- A use-of-AI-tools statement in the paper, beside competing interests.
- The P6 α-grid omissions (α = 1 is the log term itself; α = 2 is degenerate
  with t_c) are now stated in the pre-registration.
